## Content

This repository contains no instruction pages, part drawings, part renders or set photos from
any real set. The demo set (the "Pebble Rover"), its instruction book, its part numbers and
its table photo are invented and drawn by the code in `demo/`. Guides you build with the kit
contain pages of your own instruction book; keep them for your own use.

## Brickognize (optional outside service)

[Brickognize](https://brickognize.com) is a free part-recognition service run by a third
party. It is not part of this project and is not used unless you turn it on. Its own terms
apply to anything you send it; at the time of writing they allow it to keep uploaded images,
including to train its models. This project sends it only crops of single pieces (from your photo and single-part
drawings from your PDF), capped in size, each once, and logs every send.
